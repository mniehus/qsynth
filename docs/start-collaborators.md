# Collaborators

The [project roadmap](roadmap.md) shows what is established, under review, in active development, or planned. Quantum engineering currently has the most developed teaching and benchmarking sequence. Physical validation and real-time deployment remain open.

| Expertise | Entry point and current work |
|---|---|
| Stochastic thermodynamics and information theory | [Thermodynamics](thermodynamics.md). Work in progress on the scope of the work and information accounting. |
| Optical tweezers and experimental physics | [Information engine](information-engine.md). Work in progress on calibration and matching the model to experimental trajectories. |
| Control engineering | [Architecture](architecture.md). The simulator has estimator and feedback blocks. Held-out policy replay is under development. |
| FPGA / real-time systems | [FPGA implementation](fpga.md). Timing budgets and hardware-in-the-loop checks are planned. |
| Optimization | [Policy synthesis](optimization.md). Exact small-instance baselines exist; physically justified coupling and larger comparisons are under review. |
| Quantum algorithms and hardware | [Simulation and compilation](simulation.md) and [benchmarking](benchmarking.md). Synthetic-device studies are complete for the teaching case. A calibrated target and QPU execution are planned. |
