# Informatics engineering students

Start with the [project overview](overview.md) and the [system overview notebook](https://github.com/mniehus/qsynth/blob/main/notebooks/00_system_overview.ipynb). The route introduces the physics from the beginning.

1. [Information engine](information-engine.md), [stochastic thermodynamics](thermodynamics.md), and [real-time control](fpga.md) explain the bead, measurement, feedback, and work.
2. [IE cost matrix](ie-cost-matrix.md) and [policy optimization](optimization.md) connect repeated simulator runs to an exact lookup table.
3. [QUBO](qubo-ising.md), [Ising](ising.md), and [QAOA](qaoa.md) show how that objective becomes a quantum circuit.
4. [Simulation and hardware](simulation.md) and [benchmarking](benchmarking.md) distinguish algorithmic results, noisy simulation, and future device measurements.

Use the [glossary](glossary.md) as needed. The [notebook review](notebook-sequence.md) lists installation instructions, inputs, and runnable examples.

### Reproduce / explore this step

Open the [system overview](https://github.com/mniehus/qsynth/blob/main/notebooks/00_system_overview.ipynb), then run the [Bell environment check](https://github.com/mniehus/qsynth/blob/main/notebooks/00_environment_and_bell.ipynb) when you reach the quantum branch.
